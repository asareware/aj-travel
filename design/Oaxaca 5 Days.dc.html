Oaxaca de Juárez, MX
22–26 Oct
2026

Oaxaca

Five days in the valley: one market, three ruins, and as much time on foot as the afternoon heat allows. Mornings start early, afternoons stay open.

Two travelling
Base: Centro
No car until Day 4
01
Thu 22 Oct

Arrive, and one slow lap of Centro

15:00
Barrio de Jalatlaco
Drop the bags and walk the cobbles east of Centro — murals, low colour-washed walls, almost no traffic.
17:00
Templo de Santo Domingo de Guzmán
Gilded ceiling, and the Dominican family tree branching over the entrance. Late light on the stone outside.
19:30
Mezcalería In Situ
A flight of wild-agave mezcal poured with an explanation of each. Ask what came in this month.
21:00
Dinner — Los Danzantes
Courtyard tables under the brick pillars. Book ahead; ask for the patio, not the mezzanine.
02
Fri 23 Oct

Markets, then the ex-convent

08:00
Coffee at Café Brújula
Oaxacan beans, courtyard seating, open before the city is.
09:30
Mercado Benito Juárez & 20 de Noviembre
Mole pastes, chapulines, drinking chocolate ground to order. End in the pasillo de humo for grilled tasajo.
12:00
Museo de las Culturas de Oaxaca
The Tomb 7 gold from Monte Albán, held in the old Dominican convent beside Santo Domingo.
16:00
Jardín Etnobotánico
Guided visits only — book the English tour that morning. Agaves, cochineal, and a wall of cactus.
20:00
Dinner — Levadura de Olla
Thalía Barrios García cooking from her village. The small-plates menu of the day is the one to take.
03
Sat 24 Oct

Monte Albán early, clay in the afternoon

08:00
Monte Albán
Be at the gate when it opens. A Zapotec capital on a levelled ridge, with the whole valley below it. Two hours, hat on.
13:30
Comida at Itanoní
Heirloom corn, ground and cooked in the open. Order the memelas and whatever the day's tortilla is.
16:30
San Bartolo Coyotepec
Barro negro — black burnished pottery, thrown on two saucers instead of a wheel. Watch it made, then buy directly.
19:00
Drinks at Selva
Upstairs, small room, a menu built on Oaxacan botanicals. Go before nine to get a seat.
04
Sun 25 Oct

The valley, east — a full day with a car

07:30
Tlacolula Sunday market
The big one, and only on Sundays. Zapotec spoken over every stall; barbacoa for breakfast at the back.
10:30
Teotitlán del Valle
Wool rugs on pedal looms, dyed with cochineal, indigo and pericón. Ask for a dye demonstration.
13:00
Hierve el Agua
Mineral terraces over the ravine, and a spring-fed pool at the top. Swim before the tour vans arrive.
16:00
Mitla
Geometric fretwork set without mortar — thousands of cut stones held by fit alone.
18:00
A palenque near Santiago Matatlán
On the road home. Roasting pit, tahona, copper stills — whoever is open, one taste, then drive.
05
Mon 26 Oct

Slow morning, then the airport

09:00
Breakfast at Boulenc
Bread out of the oven, eggs, and the last good coffee of the trip.
10:30
Museo Textil de Oaxaca
Small, free, and the best-hung rooms in the city. Forty minutes is enough.
12:30
La Cosecha
Last lunch and last bottles — an organic market yard with mezcal worth carrying home.
15:00
OAX — Aeropuerto Xoxocotlán
Twenty-five minutes from Centro. Leave earlier than that on a Monday.
